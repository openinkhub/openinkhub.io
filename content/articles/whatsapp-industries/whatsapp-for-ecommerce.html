WhatsApp for ecommerce turns the chat app your customers already use into a sales and service channel. Shoppers can ask about sizes, browse a catalog, get cart reminders, confirm cash-on-delivery orders and track deliveries, all in one thread. For Indian online stores, where many buyers still like to "check with the seller first", WhatsApp often closes the gap between interest and purchase.

Product discovery in the chat

Many shoppers message a store before buying. They want to know if the colour is true, whether the size runs small or when stock returns. An AI chatbot can answer these from your product data and show matching items from your WhatsApp catalog. Then the shopper can add items to a cart without opening a browser.

Recovering abandoned carts

A large share of carts are left behind. A short WhatsApp reminder, sent within a few hours, often brings some of those shoppers back. Keep it simple: name the item, show the image and add a "Complete order" button. If you offer a discount, save it for the second reminder. Our abandoned cart recovery guide walks through the full setup.

Confirming COD orders

Cash on delivery is popular in India, but it brings fake orders and refusals at the door. A quick WhatsApp confirmation before dispatch helps. For example: "Hi Rahul, please confirm your COD order of ₹1,299 for 2 T-shirts. Tap Confirm or Cancel." Orders that are not confirmed can be held or called. Because return-to-origin shipments cost money both ways, even a small drop in refusals pays for the messages.

Order and delivery updates

Shoppers want to know where their parcel is. So send the key updates as utility templates:

  • Order confirmed, with the order number and items.
  • Shipped, with the courier and tracking link.
  • Out for delivery, with the expected time.
  • Delivered, with a link to rate the product.

These cut "where is my order?" calls sharply. In addition, customers can reply to any update if something goes wrong, and the chat reaches your support team.

Returns, exchanges and reviews

A return flow can ask for the order number, reason and photos, then book a pickup. Exchanges can offer the next size straight away. After delivery, a review request with one-tap star buttons collects more ratings than email. Finally, for consumables such as skincare or groceries, a reorder reminder at the right interval brings repeat sales.

Connecting your store

Shopify, WooCommerce and most custom stores can send order events to WhatsApp through an app or webhooks. Product data can sync to Meta Commerce Manager for the catalog. Then the CRM integration keeps customer records in one place.

Launching WhatsApp for ecommerce

A sensible first phase is order updates, COD confirmation and a product-question bot. Next, add cart recovery and the catalog. After that, run segmented campaigns for new arrivals and sales.
