WhatsApp for real estate helps builders, brokers and channel partners reply to property leads within seconds, share brochures and walkthrough videos, book site visits and stay in touch through long buying cycles. In property, the first agent to respond often wins the client. A WhatsApp chatbot on an official business number makes sure every lead from a portal, ad or hoarding gets an instant, useful reply, even at 11 pm.

The speed problem in property sales

Property leads arrive at all hours from portals, Facebook ads, Google ads and your website. Many buyers enquire with three or four projects at once. If your team calls back the next morning, a competitor may already have booked a site visit. So the first goal is simple: reply in seconds, and reply with something useful.

Answering portal and ad leads instantly

When a lead arrives, the system sends a WhatsApp message straight away. For example: "Hi Vikram, thanks for your interest in Green Meadows, Whitefield. Here is the brochure and price list. Would you like to see the 2BHK or 3BHK floor plans?" The buyer gets real information at once, and the conversation has started. Click-to-WhatsApp ads work even better, because the buyer opens the chat themselves.

Qualifying buyers without a long call

Next, the bot asks a few gentle questions:

  • Which configuration are you looking for?
  • What is your budget range?
  • Is this for living or investment?
  • When are you planning to buy?
  • Will you need a home loan?

The answers score the lead and route it. Hot leads go to a senior sales manager. Early-stage buyers enter a nurture sequence. All of it is saved through WhatsApp CRM integration.

Brochures, videos and virtual tours

Property is visual. In the chat, you can share floor plans, sample flat videos, drone footage, location maps and RERA details. A short video often does more than a long call. In addition, a WhatsApp catalog can list each tower or unit type with its starting price.

Booking and confirming site visits

The site visit is the key step. The bot offers weekend and weekday slots, collects the number of visitors and confirms with the sales manager's name and a map pin. A reminder goes out the evening before and again two hours before. If the buyer cannot make it, a "Reschedule" button keeps them in the funnel.

Nurturing buyers over months

Many property purchases take months. During that time, stay helpful rather than pushy. Good nurture messages include construction progress photos, new tower launches, festive offers, loan rate updates and price revision alerts. Send them once or twice a month. Then let WhatsApp retargeting bring back buyers who went quiet after a site visit.

After booking: payments and possession

WhatsApp also helps after the booking. Send demand letters, payment reminders and receipts as utility messages. Share construction updates and possession checklists. As a result, buyers feel informed, and your CRM team takes fewer calls.
