A WordPress website cost in India is usually between ₹20,000 and ₹2 lakh for the build. A simple theme-based site sits at the low end, while a custom-designed site with special features sits at the top. WooCommerce stores cost more. The WordPress software itself is free, so what you pay for is hosting, a theme, premium plugins, the design and development work, and ongoing maintenance.

The parts of a WordPress budget

Domain and hosting

A domain costs little each year. Hosting ranges from cheap shared plans to managed WordPress hosting and cloud servers. Cheap hosting is tempting, but slow or crowded servers hurt speed and SEO. For a business site, managed hosting is usually worth the extra cost.

Theme

Free themes exist, but premium themes or a custom theme give better design, support and speed. A lightweight theme matters because heavy page builders can slow the site down.

Plugins

Forms, SEO, caching, security, backups and extra features come from plugins. Many are free, while premium ones charge yearly licences. Keep the list short, because every plugin adds maintenance and risk.

Design and development

This is the main cost: planning, design, building pages, setting up plugins and testing.

Yearly running costs

  • Hosting renewal and domain renewal.
  • Premium plugin and theme licences.
  • Maintenance: updates, backups and security checks.
  • Content updates and new pages.

Why WordPress quotes vary

Two WordPress quotes can differ several times over. One may use a free theme and a page builder with no SEO work. Another may include a custom, fast theme, structured data, content help and training. So compare the details, not only the totals. In particular, ask about speed targets, SEO setup and who maintains the site after launch.

Tips to avoid overpaying

  1. Choose a well-coded theme instead of adding dozens of plugins.
  2. Prepare your text and images before the build starts.
  3. Ask for training so your team can make small edits.
  4. Get the admin login, hosting access and domain in your own name.
  5. Agree a maintenance plan before launch, not after a problem.

Is WordPress the right choice?

WordPress suits most content-led business sites and blogs. It is flexible, SEO-friendly and widely supported, which is why it powers a large share of the web according to W3Techs. However, very custom apps may suit other platforms. Read WordPress vs custom website for the comparison.
