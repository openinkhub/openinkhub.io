The best way to compare a website development company in Delhi against others is to score every shortlisted agency on the same criteria: relevant work, process, technical skill, communication, price clarity and post-launch support. Delhi NCR has thousands of options, from solo freelancers to large studios, so a simple scorecard stops you from choosing on sales pitch alone. Shortlist three to five, request itemised quotes for an identical brief, and check references before signing.

A simple agency scorecard

Give each agency a score from 1 to 5 on each row, then multiply by the weight.

CriterionWeightWhat to look for
Relevant portfolio×3Live sites in your industry that work well on mobile
Process×2Discovery, staging site, testing, clear milestones
Technical skill×2Right platform choice, speed, security, SEO basics
Communication×2Named project manager, regular updates
Price clarity×1Itemised quote, revision limits, renewals listed
Support×2Maintenance plan, response times, handover

As a result, you compare agencies on what actually affects the outcome.

The Delhi NCR market

Businesses in Delhi, Noida and Gurugram range from retailers and exporters to education, healthcare and SaaS firms. Many agencies specialise, for example in ecommerce, corporate sites or apps. Therefore, ask which industries an agency knows best, and favour one that has solved problems like yours.

How to evaluate a website development company in Delhi

  1. Send the same written brief to every agency.
  2. Ask who will actually do the work: in-house team or subcontractors.
  3. Request two client references and call them.
  4. Test their portfolio sites with PageSpeed Insights.
  5. Check that the contract gives you ownership of domain, hosting and code.

Pricing models

  • Fixed price: best when the scope is clear.
  • Time and material: suits evolving projects and apps.
  • Monthly retainer: for ongoing development and maintenance.

Red flags

First, guaranteed Google rankings. Next, no written contract or scope. Then, demands for full payment upfront. After that, an agency that cannot show live work. Finally, silence about maintenance and security after launch.

Web development in Delhi: freelancer or agency?

Web development in Delhi is offered by freelancers, small studios and large agencies. Freelancers are often cheapest and fine for a simple website design in Delhi, but cover during leave or illness can be a problem. A web development agency in Delhi usually costs more, yet offers designers, developers, testers and a project manager. Website cost in Delhi therefore varies with who you hire as much as with features. If you hire a web developer in Delhi directly, insist on the same scorecard, contract and ownership terms you would expect from an agency.

Using the scorecard in practice

After scoring, discuss the top two agencies' weak spots with them openly. For example, if support scored low, ask for a written maintenance plan. As a result, you can often improve the deal before signing.
