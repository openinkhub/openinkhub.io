The ecommerce website features that matter most are the ones that help a shopper find a product, trust you and pay without friction. That means clear product pages, fast search, a simple cart and checkout, secure local payments, reliable shipping information and a design that works on a phone. Everything else, from wishlists to loyalty points, comes after those basics are right.

Product pages that sell

A product page does the job of a salesperson. So give it what a shopper needs: several sharp photos, a zoom or short video, the price including taxes, sizes or variants, stock status, delivery estimate and a clear "Add to cart" button. Then add a description written for people, not copied from the supplier, plus reviews. Unique descriptions also help SEO, because duplicate supplier text rarely ranks.

Search, filters and navigation

As your catalog grows, navigation becomes critical. Group products into logical categories, add filters for size, price, colour and brand, and make sure search handles spelling mistakes. For example, a search for "tshirt" should find "T-shirts". Good category pages also become strong landing pages from Google.

Cart and checkout

Checkout is where most sales are lost. Keep it short: guest checkout, address autofill, PIN code delivery checks and all costs shown before payment. Avoid surprise shipping charges at the last step. In addition, let shoppers edit the cart without leaving checkout. Our guide to ecommerce conversion optimization covers more checkout fixes.

Payments and shipping for India

Offer the methods your customers use. UPI is now the default for many shoppers, while cards and wallets still matter. Cash on delivery remains important in smaller cities, but confirm COD orders before shipping to cut returns. On the shipping side, connect a courier aggregator so you can show delivery dates by PIN code and send tracking updates automatically.

Trust signals

  • Visible contact details, including phone and address.
  • Clear return, refund and shipping policies.
  • Genuine reviews and ratings.
  • Secure checkout badges from your payment gateway.
  • A real "About us" page with your story.

Features for growth

Once the basics work, add features that raise order value and repeat sales: related products, bundles, coupons, wishlists, back-in-stock alerts, loyalty points and abandoned cart reminders by email or WhatsApp. Also connect analytics so you can see which pages and campaigns sell. Google's ecommerce SEO guidance lists the structured data that helps products appear in search.

Admin features for your team

Do not forget the back office. Your team needs easy product uploads, stock management, order processing, invoice generation with GST, and reports. If you sell on marketplaces too, inventory sync saves hours every week.
