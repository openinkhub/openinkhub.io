Ecommerce website development cost in India starts at about ₹50,000 for a simple store on Shopify or WooCommerce and can reach ₹10 lakh or more for a large custom or headless store. The main factors are the platform, the number of products, custom features, integrations with inventory or ERP systems, and design. On top of the build, online stores have monthly costs: platform fees, apps, hosting, payment gateway charges and maintenance.

What pushes the cost up

  • Catalog size and complexity: many variants, bundles or custom options.
  • Custom features: product builders, subscriptions, B2B pricing or wholesale portals.
  • Integrations: ERP, inventory, accounting, marketplaces, CRM and WhatsApp.
  • Multi-vendor setups: marketplaces with seller dashboards.
  • Design: a fully custom design costs more than an adapted theme.
  • Content: product photography and descriptions for hundreds of items.

Monthly and yearly costs

  1. Platform or hosting: Shopify's plan, or hosting for WooCommerce.
  2. Apps and plugins: reviews, filters, subscriptions and more.
  3. Payment gateway: a percentage of each transaction, set by the gateway.
  4. Shipping tools: courier aggregators and label printing.
  5. Maintenance: updates, fixes and small changes.
  6. Marketing: SEO, ads, email and WhatsApp campaigns.

Many stores underestimate these. So build them into your plan from day one.

Shopify vs WooCommerce costs

Shopify is usually cheaper and faster to launch, but its monthly fees and paid apps continue for as long as the store runs. WooCommerce has no platform fee, yet you pay for hosting, plugins and upkeep. Over three years, the totals can be close. Our detailed Shopify vs WooCommerce comparison helps you decide.

How to keep costs under control

First, launch with the features that drive sales and leave extras for later. Next, avoid stacking apps you do not need, because each one adds cost and can slow the store. Then prepare product data and photos before development starts. Finally, agree a fixed scope with milestones, so changes are priced clearly.

Don't forget payment gateway fees

Gateways such as Razorpay and PayU charge a fee on each transaction, and rates vary by payment method. Check the current rates on the gateway's own site, for example Razorpay pricing, and include them in your margin calculations.
