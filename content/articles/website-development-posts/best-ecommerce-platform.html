The best ecommerce platform for a new online store is the one that fits your budget, your team's skills and the way you plan to sell. For most small businesses in India, that means Shopify or WooCommerce. Shopify suits owners who want an all-in-one setup with little maintenance. WooCommerce suits businesses that want control and strong content. Custom and headless builds make sense only when your needs outgrow both.

The main options

Shopify

A hosted platform with themes, apps and built-in checkout. You pay a monthly plan. It is quick to launch and secure by default. However, deep customisation is limited, and app fees add up.

WooCommerce

A free plugin that turns WordPress into a store. You pay for hosting, plugins and upkeep. It is very flexible and excellent for blogging. On the other hand, it needs regular updates and good hosting.

Custom ecommerce

A store built from scratch or on a framework. It fits unusual products, B2B pricing or complex integrations. It costs more and takes longer.

Headless commerce

A setup where the store engine runs behind a separate, fast front end. It suits large brands that need top speed and many channels. Read more in our headless commerce guide.

Questions to ask before you decide

  1. How many products will you sell? Ten and ten thousand need different tools.
  2. Who will run the store? A non-technical owner needs simplicity.
  3. What is your budget for year one and year three? Look beyond launch cost.
  4. Which payment and shipping partners do you need? Check they integrate.
  5. Will you sell on marketplaces too? Inventory sync becomes important.
  6. How important is content marketing? WordPress leads here.

Costs to compare

Compare the total cost over two to three years, not just setup. For Shopify, add the plan, paid apps and any transaction fees. For WooCommerce, add hosting, premium plugins, a theme and maintenance. For custom builds, add development, hosting and ongoing support. Our ecommerce cost guide gives typical Indian price ranges.

Platform features that matter in India

  • Support for Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree and UPI.
  • Cash on delivery with order confirmation.
  • GST-ready invoices.
  • Courier integrations with PIN code delivery checks.
  • Hindi or regional language support if your buyers need it.

Common mistakes

First, choosing a platform because a friend uses it. Next, picking the cheapest option without checking its limits. Then, ignoring SEO until after launch. Finally, adding dozens of apps or plugins that slow the store down. Each of these is avoidable with a short planning phase.

Our recommendation

For most new stores, start on Shopify or WooCommerce, launch quickly and learn from real sales. Move to custom or headless only when the business clearly needs it.
