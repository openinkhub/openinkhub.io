What a design package should include

  • A fixed number of pages and revision rounds.
  • A responsive design tested on phones and tablets.
  • Contact form, click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons.
  • Google Maps and your business hours.
  • Basic SEO: page titles, descriptions, headings and a sitemap.
  • Image optimisation so pages stay fast.
  • An easy CMS, often WordPress, so you can edit text yourself.

Template vs custom design

A customised template is quicker and cheaper, and it works well for many small businesses. A custom design takes longer but gives you a unique look and layouts built around your content. Either way, the design should reflect your brand rather than look like hundreds of other sites. Our guide to custom websites vs templates compares both.

How to judge design quality

  1. Open their past work on your phone first.
  2. Check whether text is easy to read and buttons are easy to tap.
  3. See whether each client's site looks different.
  4. Test how fast pages load on mobile data.
  5. Look for clear calls to action on every page.

Good design also means accessible design. The W3C accessibility introduction explains the basics, such as contrast and readable text.

Common design mistakes

First, choosing a design from a desktop screenshot without checking mobile. Next, large sliders and videos that slow the site. Then, too many fonts and colours. Finally, no clear way to contact the business.
