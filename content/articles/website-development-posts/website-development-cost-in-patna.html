Website development cost in Patna usually falls between ₹10,000 and ₹40,000 for a basic business website, ₹40,000 to ₹1.5 lakh for a larger site with more pages and SEO, and from about ₹60,000 upwards for ecommerce or custom features. Maintenance adds roughly ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month for a small site. The final price depends on pages, design, content, features and support, so always compare itemised quotes rather than headline prices.

What drives the price

  • Number of pages and how much content is needed.
  • Design: a customised template is cheaper than a fully original design.
  • Content: writing, photography and translation into Hindi add cost.
  • Features: payment gateways, booking, admission forms, multi-language.
  • Platform: WordPress is usually cheapest; custom code costs more.
  • SEO and speed work beyond the basics.
  • Support: training, maintenance and response times.

How to reduce the cost

  1. Start with the pages you truly need, then grow.
  2. Prepare your own text, photos and logo.
  3. Use a proven CMS such as WordPress instead of custom code.
  4. Add advanced features in a second phase.
  5. Choose a yearly maintenance plan rather than paying for emergencies.

Common pricing mistakes

First, choosing the cheapest quote without checking what is included. Next, forgetting yearly renewals. Then, paying everything upfront. Finally, skipping maintenance, which often costs more later when the site is hacked or breaks. If you are taking online payments, check your gateway's fees too; the Reserve Bank of India sets rules for payment aggregators.
