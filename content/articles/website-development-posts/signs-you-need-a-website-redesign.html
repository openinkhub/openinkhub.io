A website redesign is due when your site is hurting the business more than helping it. The clearest signs are a poor experience on phones, slow pages, falling enquiries or sales, a design that looks dated next to competitors, content that only a developer can update, repeated security or plugin problems, and a site that no longer matches what you sell. If several of these apply, they are the signs you need a website redesign. Plan one before they cost more traffic and revenue, and protect your SEO during the move.

Ten signs you need a website redesign

  1. It is awkward on mobile. Text is tiny, buttons are hard to tap or menus break on phones, where most Indian visitors browse.
  2. Pages load slowly. Visitors leave before the page appears, and Core Web Vitals are poor.
  3. Enquiries or sales have dropped even though traffic is steady.
  4. The design looks dated next to your competitors.
  5. Updating content is painful and every small change needs a developer.
  6. Security problems keep returning, often because the theme or plugins are no longer supported.
  7. Your business has changed: new services, new audience or a new brand.
  8. Visitors cannot find key information such as prices, location or contact details.
  9. It does not connect to your tools, such as CRM, WhatsApp, booking or payments.
  10. Accessibility is poor: low contrast, missing alt text, forms that do not work with a keyboard.

How to plan a website redesign

  1. Set clear goals, such as more enquiries, faster pages or easier editing.
  2. Audit the current site: top pages, traffic sources, rankings and conversions.
  3. Decide which content to keep, merge, rewrite or remove.
  4. Map every old URL to its new URL for 301 redirects.
  5. Design for mobile first, then build on a staging site.
  6. Test speed, forms, tracking and redirects before launch.
  7. Monitor Search Console and analytics closely for a few weeks after launch.

What a redesign costs

In India, a small business website redesign often costs ₹25,000 to ₹1.5 lakh, while large or custom sites cost more. Our website redesign cost guide breaks down the factors in detail.

Common redesign mistakes

First, changing URLs without redirects, which can wipe out years of SEO. Next, redesigning for looks alone without fixing structure and content. Then, removing pages that bring traffic. Finally, launching without testing forms and tracking.
