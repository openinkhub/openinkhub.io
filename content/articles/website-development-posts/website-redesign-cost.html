Website redesign cost in India usually runs from about half to the full price of building a new site, depending on how much changes. A visual refresh on the same platform might cost ₹20,000 to ₹80,000. A full redesign with new structure, content and technology often costs ₹80,000 to ₹3 lakh or more for a business site. Redesign when the site is slow, dated, hard to update or not bringing enquiries, and always protect your SEO during the switch.

Signs you need a redesign

  • The site loads slowly, especially on phones.
  • It looks dated next to competitors.
  • Visitors leave without enquiring or buying.
  • Your team cannot update it easily.
  • Your services, brand or audience have changed.
  • It has security issues or runs on outdated software.

What a redesign includes

  1. Audit: traffic, rankings, speed, content and what converts today.
  2. Strategy: goals, audience, new sitemap and content plan.
  3. Design: wireframes and visual designs for key pages.
  4. Build: development on the current or a new platform.
  5. Content migration: moving and improving existing pages.
  6. SEO protection: redirect map, metadata and structured data.
  7. Testing and launch: speed, forms, devices and tracking.

What affects redesign cost

The number of pages and templates matters most. Next comes content: rewriting fifty pages costs far more than moving them. Changing platform, for example from an old custom system to WordPress, adds migration work. In addition, new features such as bookings or a customer portal raise the price. On the other hand, reusing good content and photos lowers it.

Protecting SEO during a redesign

A redesign can lose traffic if it is done carelessly. To avoid that:

  • Keep URLs the same where possible.
  • Create 301 redirects from every old URL that changes.
  • Keep or improve your best-performing content.
  • Carry over titles, descriptions and schema.
  • Test on a staging site before launch.
  • Watch Search Console closely for a few weeks after.

Google explains site moves in its guide to changing URLs.

Is a redesign worth it?

A redesign is worth it when the current site holds back sales or leads. For example, a faster, clearer site can raise enquiries noticeably even with the same traffic. However, if the site works and only looks a little old, a refresh is often enough.
