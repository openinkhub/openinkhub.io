Is Laravel good for enterprise apps?

Yes. Laravel includes queues for background jobs, caching, scheduled tasks, events, robust authentication and excellent testing tools. With good architecture, a managed database and horizontal scaling, Laravel apps serve large user bases. For example, a distributor portal handling thousands of daily orders can run comfortably on Laravel with queues and caching. The official Laravel documentation shows the full feature set.

Laravel vs Django

Both are productive, full-featured frameworks. Laravel suits PHP teams, cheaper hosting and classic business apps. Django suits Python teams and projects with heavy data or AI work. Our Laravel vs Django comparison goes deeper.

How to choose a Laravel partner

  1. Ask for live Laravel apps similar to yours.
  2. Check that they use a current Laravel version and plan upgrades.
  3. Review their testing and code review process.
  4. Ask how they handle queues, caching and file storage.
  5. Confirm hosting, backups and support after launch.
  6. Make sure the repository and servers are in your name.

Costs and timelines

A small app with a few modules may take six to ten weeks. Portals with many roles, reports and integrations take three to six months. In addition, budget for hosting, monitoring and maintenance, including yearly framework upgrades.

Common mistakes

First, staying on an unsupported Laravel version for years. Next, putting business logic in controllers until they become huge. Then, running heavy tasks in web requests instead of queues. Finally, no automated tests.

Laravel alongside WordPress and other tools

Many businesses run a WordPress website for marketing and SEO, and a Laravel app for the portal or internal system behind it. This works well, because each tool does what it is best at. For example, the WordPress CMS handles blogs and landing pages, while Laravel handles customer accounts, orders and reports. In addition, a good Laravel development company keeps public pages fast so Core Web Vitals stay healthy, and connects both systems through secure APIs.
