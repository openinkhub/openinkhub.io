Website development cost in India ranges from about ₹15,000 for a basic brochure site to ₹15 lakh or more for a complex ecommerce store or custom web application. Most small and medium businesses spend between ₹30,000 and ₹3 lakh. The final price depends on the number of pages, the design approach, the features, the content and who builds it. After launch, budget for hosting, maintenance and updates every year.

What drives the price

  1. Scope. More pages, templates and features mean more work.
  2. Design. A ready theme costs less than a custom design system.
  3. Functionality. Booking, payments, member areas, calculators and integrations add cost.
  4. Content. Copywriting, photography and video are often quoted separately.
  5. SEO and speed. Proper technical SEO, schema and performance work take time.
  6. Team. Freelancers, small studios and large agencies price differently.

Freelancer vs agency pricing

A freelancer is often the cheapest option for a small site. However, you depend on one person for design, code, SEO and support. An agency costs more but brings a team, a process and backup if someone is unavailable. Our comparison of a website development agency vs a freelancer covers the trade-offs.

Yearly running costs

  • Domain: usually under ₹1,500 a year for a .com or .in.
  • Hosting: from a few thousand rupees a year for shared hosting to much more for cloud servers.
  • Maintenance: often ₹1,500 to ₹25,000 a month, depending on scope.
  • Plugins, apps and licences: varies by platform.
  • Email and tools: business email, analytics and marketing tools.

A common rule of thumb is to budget 15% to 20% of the build cost each year for upkeep. See our website maintenance cost guide for detail.

How to compare quotes

Ask every agency for a line-by-line scope. Then check: how many pages and templates; whether content, images and SEO are included; who owns the code and domain; what support is included after launch; and whether GST is included. Two quotes that look far apart are often for very different work.

Ways to control the budget

Launch a focused first version, then add features once the site proves itself. Prepare your content early, because delays cost money. Also choose a platform your team can update, so you do not pay for every small change.
