A Shopify development agency designs, builds and improves online stores on Shopify. Typical work includes store setup, theme customisation or custom theme development, apps and integrations, migration from another platform, speed improvements and conversion optimisation. To choose the right one, review live stores they have built, ask about migrations and redirects, check how they keep stores fast with many apps, and confirm post-launch support. In India a theme-based Shopify store often costs ₹30,000 to ₹1.5 lakh, plus Shopify's own plan and app fees.

How to choose a Shopify agency

  1. Open three of their live stores on your phone and test checkout up to payment.
  2. Ask which apps they would use for your needs and why.
  3. Check how they handle speed when several apps are installed.
  4. Ask about Indian payment gateways, COD and shipping integrations.
  5. Confirm that the store and all app accounts are in your name.
  6. Agree support, training and response times after launch.

Shopify migration

A good Shopify development agency can migrate products, customers and order history from WooCommerce, Magento or other platforms. Most importantly, it maps every old URL to its new Shopify URL with 301 redirects, so search rankings are kept. Payments, emails, taxes and shipping must be tested on a staging copy before launch. Shopify's migration guide lists the main steps.

Shopify vs WooCommerce

Shopify is fully hosted, secure and easy for a small team to run. WooCommerce gives more control and flexibility but needs hosting and regular maintenance. Our Shopify vs WooCommerce guide compares them in detail.

What Shopify development costs

Theme-based stores are at the lower end. Custom themes, complex product options, B2B pricing or ERP integrations raise the cost. In addition, include Shopify's monthly plan, paid apps and transaction fees where they apply. Therefore, compare agencies on the total first-year cost, not just the build price. Common mistakes

First, installing too many apps, which slows the store. Next, copying product descriptions from suppliers, which hurts SEO. Then, skipping redirects during migration. Finally, not testing checkout on mobile, where most orders happen.

Hiring Shopify developers vs an agency

You can hire Shopify developers as freelancers for small theme changes. However, a full store build or migration usually needs design, development, SEO and testing together, which an agency covers. In addition, an agency can usually support you during busy sale seasons. Whichever you choose, insist on a staging theme for changes, so the live store is never broken by testing. Shopify customization should always be tested on mobile before it is published.
