Step-by-step: choosing an agency

  1. Write a one-page brief: goals, pages, features, budget and deadline.
  2. Shortlist three agencies from referrals, Google reviews and their own websites.
  3. Check live work, not screenshots; test speed and forms on a phone.
  4. Call two past clients and ask about deadlines and support.
  5. Compare itemised quotes for the same scope.
  6. Agree a written contract covering payments, timelines, revisions and ownership.

Questions to ask a website development company in Patna

  • Which platform will you use, such as WordPress or Shopify, and why?
  • Will the domain and hosting be registered in my business name?
  • How many design revisions are included?
  • Is basic SEO, such as titles, sitemap and Google Business Profile linking, included?
  • Who will maintain the site after launch, and at what monthly cost?
  • Can I edit text and photos myself?

Red flags

First, a very low price with no written scope. Next, the domain registered in the agency's name. Then, no live portfolio. After that, pirated or "nulled" themes, which often hide malware. Finally, promises of first-page rankings within weeks.

Local SEO matters

A Patna website should support local search: correct name, address and phone details, a linked Google Business Profile and pages for your main services. Google explains how local results work in its local ranking guide.
