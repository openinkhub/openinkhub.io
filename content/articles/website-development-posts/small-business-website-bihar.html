Must-have features

  1. Mobile-friendly design that loads fast on 4G.
  2. Click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons on every page.
  3. Google Map, address and opening hours.
  4. Clear services with prices or price ranges.
  5. Real photos and customer reviews.
  6. A short enquiry form.
  7. Hindi content where your customers prefer it.

Local SEO in Bihar

Local SEO helps your small business website appear when nearby customers search. Start with a verified Google Business Profile, keep your name, address and phone the same everywhere, add a page for each main service and ask happy customers for reviews. Google's own guide to local rankings explains the main factors: relevance, distance and prominence.

What it costs

A simple site often costs ₹10,000 to ₹40,000, while sites with more pages, local SEO and booking or payment features cost more. Add domain, hosting and maintenance each year. Our website development cost in Patna guide lists typical ranges.

Common mistakes

First, relying only on a Facebook page that you do not control. Next, a site with no phone number or WhatsApp button above the fold. Then, outdated prices and timings. Finally, building on a cheap template that is slow and never updated.

A simple plan to get started

First, list the three questions customers ask you most often, such as price, location and timings. Next, make sure the website answers them on the home page. Then, connect the site to your Google Business Profile and WhatsApp number. After that, add a few real photos of your shop, clinic or classroom. Finally, ask five happy customers for Google reviews. These steps cost very little, yet they make a new small business website in Bihar far more useful from the first week.
