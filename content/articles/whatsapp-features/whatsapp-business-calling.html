WhatsApp business calling adds voice to your WhatsApp support. Customers can tap a call button in the chat to reach your team, and agents can call customers back after the customer agrees. The call happens inside WhatsApp, over the internet, so there is no phone bill for the customer. Most importantly, the call sits next to the chat history, so agents know the context before they speak.

What WhatsApp business calling is

Meta added calling to the WhatsApp Business Platform so businesses can talk to customers without switching apps. Calls are routed to your software, not to one phone. That means you can send them to the right team, queue them and track them, just like chats. For technical details, see Meta's calling documentation.

Customer-initiated vs business-initiated calls

Customer calls you

A call button appears on your business profile or in a message. The customer taps it, and the call rings your agents. This suits support lines and sales enquiries.

You call the customer

A business cannot cold-call on WhatsApp. First, it asks for permission in the chat, for example with a "Call me" button. Once the customer agrees, an agent can place the call. This keeps calling personal and avoids spam.

When voice beats text

  • Complex questions that need back-and-forth, such as loan terms or a technical fault.
  • High-value sales where trust matters, such as property, cars or education.
  • Upset customers who want to be heard.
  • Older customers who prefer to talk rather than type.
  • Language needs, where a short call is quicker than long messages.

However, voice should be an option, not the default. Most simple questions are still faster by text, and an AI bot can answer many of them instantly.

Setting up call routing

  1. Decide which numbers and teams take calls, and during which hours.
  2. Set a fallback for missed calls, such as an automatic message offering a callback.
  3. Add a "Call us" button to key flows, for instance after a bot hand-off.
  4. Train agents to note the outcome of each call in the chat.

Also tell customers your calling hours in the business profile. Outside those hours, the bot can take a callback request.

Linking calls to the chat history

The real value of calling inside WhatsApp is context. When a customer calls, the agent sees the earlier chat, tags and order details in the team inbox. After the call, notes and outcomes stay in the same thread. Then everything syncs to your CRM through CRM integration. As a result, no one has to ask "can you explain again?"

Costs and privacy

For the customer, a WhatsApp call uses mobile data, like any WhatsApp call. For the business, Meta sets calling prices, and they can change, so check the current rate card before launch. If you record calls, tell customers and follow your privacy policy and local law.
