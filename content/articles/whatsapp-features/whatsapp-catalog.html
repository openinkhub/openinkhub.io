A WhatsApp catalog lets customers browse your products inside the chat, add items to a cart and send the order to you, without leaving WhatsApp. On the WhatsApp Business Platform, the catalog is connected to Meta Commerce Manager, so it can stay in sync with your online store. For many Indian shoppers who already chat with sellers on WhatsApp, this turns a conversation into a sale in a few taps.

What a WhatsApp catalog is

Think of the catalog as a mini storefront attached to your business number. Each item has a name, image, price, description and link. Customers can open it from your profile or from a product message. Then they pick items, adjust quantities and send the cart as an order message. Your team or bot confirms the order, takes payment and arranges delivery.

Connecting your product data

  1. Create a catalog in Meta Commerce Manager inside your business portfolio.
  2. Add products manually, by spreadsheet, or with a data feed from your store.
  3. Connect the catalog to your WhatsApp Business Account.
  4. Turn on the cart so customers can place multi-item orders.

For Shopify, WooCommerce and most custom stores, a feed keeps prices and stock updated automatically. Meta's guide to selling products on WhatsApp explains catalog setup in detail.

Single and multi-product messages

Besides the full catalog, you can send products directly in a chat:

  • Single-product message: one item with image, price and a "View" button. This is useful when a customer asks about a specific product.
  • Multi-product message: up to a set number of items grouped into sections, such as "New arrivals" or "Under ₹999".
  • Catalog message: opens your whole catalog from a button.

An AI WhatsApp bot can pick the right products based on what the customer typed, for example "black running shoes size 9".

From cart to order

When a customer sends a cart, the order arrives in your inbox with the items and total. Next, the bot or agent confirms delivery details. Then payment can be collected with a payment link from your gateway, or as cash on delivery. Finally, order updates go out as utility templates: confirmed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered. For COD orders, a quick confirmation message before dispatch helps reduce returns. Our WhatsApp for ecommerce guide covers the full journey.

Keeping stock and prices in sync

Nothing frustrates a customer more than ordering an item that is out of stock. So a live feed from your store is worth the setup. Also review the catalog monthly for broken images, old prices and hidden items. In addition, check that every product is allowed under WhatsApp's commerce policy, which restricts items such as alcohol, weapons and some health products.

Using the catalog in campaigns

Catalog messages work well in campaigns. For example, a festive broadcast can carry a multi-product message with your best sellers. Likewise, a retargeting message can show the exact item a customer left behind. As a result, the path from message to purchase stays short.
