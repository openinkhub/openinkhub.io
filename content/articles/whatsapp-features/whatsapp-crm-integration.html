WhatsApp CRM integration connects your WhatsApp number to your CRM, so every new lead, chat, tag and status update is saved automatically. It also works the other way: a change in the CRM, such as a deal moving stage, can send a WhatsApp message. As a result, sales and support teams work from one record, and no lead is lost in someone's phone.

Why sync WhatsApp with your CRM

  • No lost leads: every new contact becomes a CRM record within seconds.
  • Full context: agents see past orders, deals and notes while they chat.
  • Less copying: nobody types chat details into the CRM by hand.
  • Better follow-up: CRM rules can trigger WhatsApp reminders and nudges.
  • Clear reporting: you can see which leads came from WhatsApp and what they bought.

What data to sync

Start with the basics, then add more only if your team uses it. A typical setup syncs:

  1. Name, phone number and opt-in status.
  2. Lead source, such as an ad, website button or QR code.
  3. Answers collected by the bot, such as city, budget or product.
  4. Tags and the assigned agent.
  5. A chat summary or the full transcript.
  6. Lead stage and outcome.

Opt-in status is the most important field. It decides who can receive marketing templates. So make sure it updates when someone opts out.

Mapping fields correctly

Field mapping decides where each piece of WhatsApp data lands in your CRM. For example, the bot's "budget" answer maps to the CRM's budget field, and the "city" answer maps to the address. Also decide how to match records. Usually the phone number is the key, formatted the same way in both systems, for instance with the +91 country code. Otherwise, you will create duplicates.

Triggering messages from CRM events

Once the systems talk, the CRM can start WhatsApp conversations. Common triggers include:

  • a new lead assigned, which sends a welcome and the salesperson's name;
  • a meeting booked, which sends a confirmation and a reminder;
  • a deal stuck at one stage, which sends a gentle follow-up;
  • a payment due, which sends a reminder with a payment link.

Each of these uses an approved template from your template library. For automated follow-ups, see WhatsApp retargeting.

Common CRMs we connect

Most CRMs with an API or webhooks can connect to WhatsApp. We regularly work with Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Salesforce, LeadSquared, Google Sheets for small teams and custom CRMs built in-house. If your CRM has no direct connector, a webhook or automation platform can bridge the two. HubSpot, for instance, documents its options in its developer API overview.

Keeping data clean and safe

First, sync only what you need. Next, limit who can see full chat transcripts. Then set a retention rule, so old chats and documents do not pile up forever. Finally, test the sync each time either system changes, because a renamed field can quietly break the flow.
