To send a WhatsApp broadcast message, you have two options. In the WhatsApp Business App, you create a broadcast list of up to 256 contacts and send one message that each person receives privately. On the WhatsApp Business API, you choose an approved template and an opted-in audience, and send to thousands at once. The app is fine for small lists. The API is the right choice for anything larger or regular.

How to send a WhatsApp broadcast message in the Business App

  1. Open WhatsApp Business and tap the menu or the new chat icon.
  2. Choose New broadcast.
  3. Select up to 256 contacts for the list and confirm.
  4. Type your message, add an image or document if needed, and send.

Each person receives the message as a normal one-to-one chat. However, there is a catch: only contacts who have saved your number in their phone will receive it. So a large share of your list may never see the message. Also, replies arrive as separate chats, which you answer one by one. Menu names can change between app versions, so look for "broadcast" if the label differs.

How to send a broadcast on the WhatsApp API

  1. Prepare the audience. Import or select opted-in contacts, ideally by segment.
  2. Pick a template. Choose an approved marketing or utility template, or create one and wait for approval.
  3. Fill the variables. Map fields such as name or order number to each contact.
  4. Schedule or send. Send now, or schedule for the best time.
  5. Handle replies. Route replies to a chatbot or your team inbox.

On the API, contacts do not need to save your number. In addition, your verified business name appears, and you get delivery and read reports. Our WhatsApp broadcast messaging page explains how this works at scale.

Writing a broadcast that gets replies

Keep it short and personal. Open with the customer's name, say why you are writing and end with one action. For example: "Hi Kavya, our new monsoon collection is live. As a member, you get free delivery this week. Tap below to browse." Avoid sending the same broadcast every few days, because people will block the number. Our template examples page has more wording ideas.

Checking the results

After sending, look at how many messages were delivered, read and replied to. On the API, also check button clicks and blocks. If delivery is low, your list may contain wrong numbers or people not on WhatsApp. If blocks are high, the message or audience needs work. Then adjust before the next send.

Staying within the rules

Whether you use the app or the API, only message people who agreed to hear from you. Large unsolicited broadcasts from the app can get your number banned. On the API, Meta's opt-in and template rules apply. Our bulk WhatsApp messages guide covers safe sending in detail.
