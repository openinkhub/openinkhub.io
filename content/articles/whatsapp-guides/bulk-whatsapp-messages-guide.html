You can send bulk WhatsApp messages safely only through the official WhatsApp Business API, to people who opted in, using approved templates. Unofficial bulk senders, browser extensions and "WhatsApp Web" tools break WhatsApp's terms, and numbers that use them are often banned within days. The official route takes a little setup, but it lets you send thousands of personalised messages with your verified business name and full delivery reports.

Why bulk senders get numbers banned

WhatsApp watches for spam patterns. Unofficial tools typically send the same text to many unknown numbers in a short time, from a normal WhatsApp account. Recipients block and report the number, and WhatsApp's systems act quickly. In addition, these tools often need your phone to stay connected, they cannot use templates, and they offer no proper opt-out. So even if a tool works today, the risk grows with every send. The official way to send bulk WhatsApp messages

  1. Get API access. Register your number on the WhatsApp Cloud API, ideally with a verified Meta business portfolio.
  2. Collect opt-in. Every contact must have agreed to receive WhatsApp messages from you.
  3. Create templates. Write and submit the message as a template, in the right category.
  4. Upload your audience. Import opted-in contacts with any personal fields you need.
  5. Send in stages. Start with a small batch, check results, then send the rest.

Preparing your contact list

A clean list protects your quality rating. Before sending:

  • Remove duplicates and numbers without a country code.
  • Remove anyone who opted out, on any channel.
  • Check where each contact came from, and drop any without clear consent.
  • Split the list by language, city or interest if you can.

Format numbers consistently, for example +91 followed by ten digits. Otherwise, some messages will fail.

Sending in stages

New numbers have a daily limit on how many customers they can message first. The limit rises as you send good-quality messages. So do not try to reach 50,000 people on day one. Instead, start with your most engaged contacts, who are least likely to block you. Then expand over days or weeks while watching the quality rating in WhatsApp Manager. Personalising each message

Bulk does not have to mean generic. Templates can include the customer's name, last purchase, city or appointment time. They can also carry an image, a document and buttons. A personal message with one clear action earns far more replies than a mass blast. As a result, your cost per result falls even though the message cost stays the same.

After the send

Replies will come in, often within minutes. Have a chatbot or agents ready. Then check the report: delivered, read, replied, clicked and blocked. If blocks are high, the message or the audience was wrong. Fix that before the next send. See WhatsApp analytics for what to track.
