These WhatsApp message template examples cover the messages most businesses need: welcome, order, appointment, payment, feedback, offers, win-back and one-time passcodes. Each example shows the category it belongs to and uses numbered variables such as {{1}} for details like the customer's name. Adapt the wording to your brand, add clear sample values when you submit, and you will have a strong starting library.

How to use these WhatsApp message template examples

  1. Pick the example closest to what you need.
  2. Change the wording to match how your brand speaks.
  3. Choose the right category: utility, marketing or authentication.
  4. Add realistic sample values for each variable when submitting.
  5. Add buttons where they help the customer act.

Authentication template example

"{{1}} is your verification code. For your security, do not share this code." Button: Copy code. Authentication templates follow a fixed format set by Meta, so you mostly choose options rather than writing free text.

Tips for getting templates approved

  • Never start or end a message with a variable.
  • Give each variable enough text around it to make its meaning clear.
  • Keep utility templates free of offers, or they will be classed as marketing.
  • Avoid all-caps, too many emojis and vague text such as "Click here".
  • Do not ask for card numbers, PINs or passwords.

Meta's template guidelines list the current rules.

Languages

If your customers speak Hindi or another regional language, submit each template in that language too, under the same template name. Have a native speaker check the tone, because a literal translation can sound stiff.
