WhatsApp abandoned cart recovery sends a shopper a short, timely reminder after they leave items in their cart without paying. Because WhatsApp messages are usually seen within minutes, a well-timed reminder brings back a share of shoppers who would ignore an email. Setting it up takes four parts: opt-in at checkout, a trigger from your store, one or two approved templates and a report on recovered revenue.

Why carts are abandoned

Shoppers leave carts for ordinary reasons. They get distracted, compare prices, wait for payday, doubt the size or see a surprise delivery charge. Some never meant to buy. So a reminder will not save every cart. However, it does catch the shoppers who were interrupted or unsure, and those are often a large group.

Step 1: capture WhatsApp opt-in

You can only message shoppers who agreed to it. The best place to ask is early in checkout, when the shopper enters their phone number. Add a clear tick box such as "Send me order updates and reminders on WhatsApp". Many stores also capture opt-in from a "Chat on WhatsApp" button or a pop-up. Cart reminders are marketing messages, so the opt-in should cover marketing, not just order updates.

Step 2: connect the trigger

Your store needs to tell the WhatsApp platform when a cart is abandoned. Shopify and WooCommerce apps, or a webhook from a custom store, can send the event with the shopper's name, items, cart value and a checkout link. Then the automation waits for a set time and checks whether the order was completed before sending anything.

Step 3: time the reminders

  1. First reminder: one to four hours after abandonment. Simple and helpful, with no discount.
  2. Second reminder (optional): about 24 hours later. This is where a small incentive, such as free shipping, can help.

Stop there. More reminders tend to raise blocks. Also stop the sequence the moment the shopper buys or replies.

Step 4: write the messages

Show the product image, name the item and add one button that opens the checkout. For example:

  • "Hi {{1}}, you left the {{2}} in your cart. It is still available in your size. Tap below to finish your order."
  • "Still thinking it over, {{1}}? Your cart is saved, and delivery is free if you order today."

Add a "Talk to us" button too. Sometimes the shopper has a question about size or delivery, and a quick answer saves the sale. A WhatsApp AI bot can handle many of these. For more message ideas, see our template examples.

Using discounts carefully

If every reminder includes a coupon, regular customers learn to abandon carts on purpose. So keep the first message offer-free. Use a discount only in the second reminder, or only for high-value carts or first-time buyers. Then test whether the discount actually lifts recovery enough to cover its cost.

Measuring WhatsApp abandoned cart recovery

  • Recovery rate: recovered carts divided by reminders sent.
  • Recovered revenue: the value of orders completed after a reminder.
  • Cost: message charges plus any discounts given.
  • Block rate: a sign the timing or tone is off.
