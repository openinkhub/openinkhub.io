To unban a WhatsApp number, open WhatsApp on the banned number, tap the option to request a review, explain briefly why you think the ban is a mistake and wait for WhatsApp's decision. Only WhatsApp can lift a ban, and there is no paid service that can do it for you. If the ban is upheld, the number usually cannot be used on WhatsApp again, which is why preventing a ban matters far more than appealing one.

Why WhatsApp numbers get banned

WhatsApp bans numbers that break its terms of service. For businesses, the common causes are:

  • sending bulk messages from the regular or Business App to people who did not opt in;
  • using unofficial bulk senders, auto-clickers or modified WhatsApp apps;
  • many recipients blocking or reporting the number in a short time;
  • adding people to groups without permission;
  • selling products banned under WhatsApp's commerce policy;
  • scraping contacts or sending automated messages without the API.

In most business cases, the root cause is messaging people at scale without permission.

Temporary vs permanent bans

A temporary ban usually appears as a countdown in the app. It often follows the use of an unofficial app or a burst of reports. After the timer ends, the number works again. However, repeat behaviour can turn it into a permanent ban. A permanent ban shows a message that the number is no longer allowed to use WhatsApp. That is the case where a review request matters.

How to unban a WhatsApp number in the app

  1. Open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business on the banned number.
  2. Tap the option to request a review, often labelled Request a review or Support.
  3. Verify the number with the code sent by SMS.
  4. Write a short, honest explanation. For example, explain that you only message existing customers and will stop any practice that caused the ban.
  5. Submit, then wait. Avoid sending repeated requests.

You can also contact WhatsApp support through its Help Center. If you used an unofficial app, uninstall it and switch to the official one before appealing.

API account restrictions

On the WhatsApp Business Platform, enforcement looks different. Instead of a sudden ban, you may see a falling quality rating, paused templates, a lower messaging limit or a restriction on your WhatsApp Business Account. Preventing bans in future

  • Use the official API for anything beyond personal-scale messaging. See WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Collect opt-in before you message anyone first.
  • Send useful, relevant messages to segmented audiences.
  • Offer an easy opt-out in every marketing message.
  • Grow volume gradually and watch your quality rating.
  • Never buy contact lists.

Beware of "unban services"

Some people sell services that claim to unban a WhatsApp number for a fee. They cannot. At best they file the same review you can file yourself. At worst they ask for your verification code and take over your account. Never share your WhatsApp code with anyone.

If the ban is upheld

If WhatsApp confirms the ban, you will need a new number. Before you start again, fix the cause. Set up the official API, rebuild your opt-in list and plan your messaging properly.
