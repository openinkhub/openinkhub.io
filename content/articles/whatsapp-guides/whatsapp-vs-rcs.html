WhatsApp vs RCS is a choice between two rich messaging channels. WhatsApp is an app that most Indian smartphone users already have, with two-way chat, chatbots, catalogs and payments. RCS, or Rich Communication Services, upgrades the phone's default SMS inbox with images, buttons and a verified sender name, without needing a separate app. For most Indian businesses, WhatsApp is the main conversation channel, while RCS is a useful upgrade for one-way alerts and SMS fallback.

What RCS is

RCS is the successor to SMS. On supported Android phones, messages arrive in the default messaging app, such as Google Messages, with rich features: images, carousels, suggested reply buttons, read receipts and a branded, verified sender. Apple added RCS support to iPhones with iOS 18, although business messaging availability depends on the carrier and country. Where RCS is not available, messages can fall back to plain SMS.

Reach in India

WhatsApp's reach in India is hard to beat. Most smartphone users already use it every day, and many expect to chat with shops and service providers there. By contrast, RCS reach depends on the phone model, the carrier and whether the feature is turned on. It is growing, especially on Android. However, for now, WhatsApp is the safer bet for conversations. Our WhatsApp statistics page lists the user figures.

When to use WhatsApp

  • Customer support and sales conversations.
  • Chatbots that answer questions or book slots.
  • Product catalogs and orders in the chat.
  • Campaigns to an opted-in audience, with replies handled by a team inbox.

When to use RCS

  • One-way alerts where you would otherwise use SMS, such as delivery or bank alerts.
  • Reaching customers who are not on WhatsApp.
  • Branded OTP and transactional messages with SMS fallback.

Using both channels together

Many brands run both. WhatsApp handles conversations, campaigns and service. RCS, with SMS fallback, handles alerts and covers people who do not use WhatsApp. If you go this route, keep consent records for each channel and avoid sending the same message on both, which annoys customers.
