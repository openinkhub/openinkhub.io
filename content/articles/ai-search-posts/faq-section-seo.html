FAQ section SEO is about adding a short block of real questions and direct answers to a page so it matches more searches, answers leftover doubts and gives AI tools quotable text. The best practice is simple: three to eight genuine questions per page, each answered in one to three sentences, placed near the end before the final call to action.

Best practices for an FAQ section

  • Use questions people actually ask, taken from sales, support or People Also Ask.
  • Answer in the first sentence, then add one useful detail.
  • Keep each question distinct; never repeat the main heading's question.
  • Write questions in the reader's words, not internal terms.
  • Update answers when prices, laws or features change.
  • Use HTML headings or a clear list so engines can parse the block.

20 FAQ examples by page type

Service page

  1. How much does this service cost per month?
  2. How long before I see results?
  3. Do you sign a long contract?
  4. What do you need from me to start?
  5. Will I get reports, and how often?

Blog post or guide

  1. Is this method still allowed by Google?
  2. Can a beginner do this without tools?
  3. How often should I repeat this?
  4. What is the most common mistake?
  5. Does this work for local businesses too?

Product or pricing page

  1. Can I change plans later?
  2. Are taxes included in the price?
  3. Is there a free trial or demo?
  4. What happens if I cancel?
  5. Which plan suits a small team?

Local business page

  1. Which areas do you serve?
  2. What are your opening hours?
  3. Do you offer home or site visits?
  4. How do I book an appointment?
  5. Is parking available nearby?

How to write strong FAQ answers

Start with "Yes", "No" or the key fact when you can. For example, "Yes, you can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice" beats "Cancellation policies vary". Then add one line of context. Also, avoid stuffing keywords into questions, since it reads badly and helps nobody.

The same approach powers answer-first content across the whole page.

Where to place the FAQ section

Good FAQ section SEO also depends on position. Place it after the main content and before the final call to action. That way it catches doubts just before the reader decides. On long guides, a short FAQ near the end also gives skimmers a quick summary.

Does FAQ section SEO still work?

Yes, though the reward has changed. Since 2023, Google shows FAQ rich results for very few sites, so an FAQ block rarely changes how your listing looks. Still, the questions help pages match long-tail searches, and clear answers give AI Overviews and assistants text to quote. Our analysis of FAQ schema explains what changed. Google's own note on the HowTo and FAQ rich result changes gives the official detail.

Common FAQ mistakes

  • Writing questions nobody asks, just to add keywords.
  • Copying the same FAQ block onto many pages.
  • Hiding answers that matter, such as price, deep in an accordion.
  • Letting answers contradict the main page content.
