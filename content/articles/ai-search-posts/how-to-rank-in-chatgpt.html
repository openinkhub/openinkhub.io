How to rank in ChatGPT: allow OpenAI's search crawler to reach your site, publish clear pages that answer buyer questions directly, keep your brand facts consistent across the web, and earn mentions on the sites ChatGPT already cites for your topic. There is no paid placement and no secret tag. ChatGPT search picks sources it can fetch, understand and trust.

How ChatGPT search finds sources

When a question needs fresh information, ChatGPT runs a web search and reads a set of pages. It then writes an answer and, in many cases, shows links to the sources. For other questions, it answers from what the model learned in training, which reflects how often and how consistently the web discussed a brand.

So learning how to rank in ChatGPT means working on both routes: pages that retrieval can use, and a brand the model already associates with your topic.

Step 1: Allow the right crawler

OpenAI lists separate bots in its crawler documentation. First, OAI-SearchBot gathers pages for ChatGPT search results. Second, GPTBot collects content for model training. Finally, ChatGPT-User fetches pages when a user asks ChatGPT to open a link. If you block OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt, your pages may not appear in ChatGPT search answers. Step 2: Make pages easy to read

Also, many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript. So check that your main text, prices and headings appear in the raw HTML. Then open each section with a direct answer, use tables for comparisons and keep facts current. See JavaScript SEO for AI and answer-first content for the detail.

Step 3: Cover the questions buyers ask

Buyers ask ChatGPT things like "which CRM suits a 10-person sales team?" or "is it safe to switch web hosts?". List those questions, then make sure one page answers each clearly. Comparison pages are especially useful, since they match "which is better" prompts.

Step 4: Build consistent brand facts

In practice, ChatGPT describes brands based on what it reads. If your site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn page and directory listings give different service lists or locations, the model may describe you wrongly. Fix them so they match. Our guide to entity SEO covers the full process.

Step 5: Earn mentions where ChatGPT looks

Run your main buyer questions in ChatGPT and note the cited sources. In fact, they are often industry blogs, review sites, "best of" lists, Reddit threads and news sites. Then work to earn a fair place in those sources, through PR, expert quotes, reviews and genuine community answers. Our guide to brand mentions in LLM answers gives seven ways to start.

Step 6: Track your visibility

Build a fixed list of 30 prompts and run them monthly, as explained in our AI prompt set guide. Then record mentions, citations and how ChatGPT describes you. In addition, track visits from chatgpt.com in analytics, using the method in our guide to AI traffic in GA4.

What does not work

  • Hidden text or prompts aimed at AI crawlers.
  • Mass-produced pages that repeat the same template.
  • Buying fake reviews or spam mentions.
  • Services that promise a guaranteed ChatGPT ranking.

Overall, these tactics either fail or create risk with search engines too. Instead, focus on being the clearest, most trusted answer in your niche.
