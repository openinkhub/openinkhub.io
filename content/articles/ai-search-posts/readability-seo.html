Readability SEO is about writing pages that people can read and understand quickly, using measures such as the Flesch reading ease score, sentence length and passive voice as guides. On its own, readability is not a direct Google ranking factor. However, clear writing keeps readers on the page, helps them act and makes your answers easier for search engines and AI tools to quote.

The Flesch reading ease score

The Flesch score rates text from 0 to 100 based on average sentence length and syllables per word. Higher means easier. Most business content reads well at 50 to 70. Technical or legal topics may score lower, which is fine if the audience expects it. Still, a very low score usually signals long sentences and heavy words that you can trim.

Sentence length

Aim for an average of 15 to 20 words per sentence, and keep sentences over 20 words to less than a quarter of the total. Long sentences are not wrong, but too many in a row tire readers. So split sentences that join two ideas with "and" or "which". Then vary the rhythm: a short sentence after a long one helps the point land.

Passive voice

Passive voice hides who does what, as in "The report was sent." Active voice is clearer: "We sent the report." Keep passive sentences under about 10 percent. Use passive only when the actor does not matter or is unknown, such as "The site was built in 2019."

Other readability checks

  • Paragraph length: keep most paragraphs under 100 words.
  • Headings: add a heading at least every 250 to 300 words.
  • Transition words: words such as "so", "however" and "for example" link ideas and guide readers.
  • Varied openings: avoid starting several sentences in a row with the same word.
  • Plain words: prefer "use" to "utilise" and "help" to "facilitate".
  • Lists and tables: turn steps and comparisons into structured formats.

How to edit for readability

  1. Read the draft aloud. Where you stumble, readers will too.
  2. Cut warm-up phrases and filler words.
  3. Split long sentences and long paragraphs.
  4. Swap passive constructions for active ones.
  5. Replace jargon with plain words, or explain it once.
  6. Check the score with a readability tool, but trust your judgement.

Readability SEO and AI answers

AI tools quote passages that state a fact clearly in one or two sentences. Long, winding paragraphs are harder to extract and easier to misread. As a result, readable pages tend to be quoted more accurately. Our guide to answer-first content shows how to structure sections for this.

Do not chase scores blindly

A tool can flag a long sentence, but it cannot judge whether your point is clear. So use scores as prompts, not rules. Some topics need precise terms, and that is fine. The goal is that your reader understands quickly, not that a number turns green.
