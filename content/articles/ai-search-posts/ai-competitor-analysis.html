AI competitor analysis shows which competitors AI assistants mention for your buyer questions, which sources those answers cite, and where you are missing. The output is a citation gap list: prompts and sources where rivals appear and you do not. Closing those gaps, one by one, is the most direct way to grow your share of AI answers.

Why classic competitor analysis is not enough

Traditional SEO competitor research compares rankings, keywords and backlinks. That still matters. However, AI answers often name brands that do not rank first, because they appear in reviews, lists and forums the assistant trusts. So you need a second view: who the AI recommends, and why.

The citation gap template

Build a sheet with one row per prompt and these columns:

ColumnWhat to record
Prompt IDFrom your frozen prompt set
ToolChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot
You mentioned?Yes or no
Competitors namedList each brand
Sources citedURLs or domains
Gap typeContent, source or entity
Next actionThe fix you will make

Use the same prompts each month, as described in our guide to the AI prompt set.

Step 1: Choose your competitors

Pick three to five brands. Include your closest direct rivals, plus one or two that AI tools mention often even if you do not see them as rivals. Those surprise names often reveal which sources the assistants trust.

Step 2: Run the prompts

Run each prompt in each tool, ideally three times, since answers vary. Then record every brand and every cited source. A logged-out or clean session reduces personal bias.

Step 3: Classify each gap

  • Content gap: you have no page that answers the prompt well. Fix it by creating or rewriting a page, using answer-first content.
  • Source gap: the answer cites a list, review site or forum where competitors appear and you do not. Fix it by earning a fair place there.
  • Entity gap: the AI knows little about you or describes you wrongly. Fix it with the steps in our guide to entity SEO.

Step 4: Prioritise

Next, score each gap by buyer intent and effort. High-intent prompts, such as "best SEO agency for a clinic in Kolkata", come first. Then pick the quick fixes: a missing comparison page or an outdated directory listing often takes hours, not months.

Step 5: Study the winning sources

Open the sources cited most often. Note what they have in common: maybe fresh dates, comparison tables or real customer quotes. That tells you what the assistants value in your niche. Our guide to comparison content helps if tables keep appearing.

Step 6: Repeat monthly

Run the analysis each month and track your mention rate against each competitor. Over time, the gap list should shrink. If a rival suddenly gains ground, check which new sources cite them.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Changing prompts every month, which breaks the trend.
  • Counting a single run as the truth.
  • Copying competitor content instead of answering better.
  • Ignoring the sources and only looking at brand names.
