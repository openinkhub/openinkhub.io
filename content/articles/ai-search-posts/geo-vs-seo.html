GEO vs SEO: SEO helps your pages rank and earn clicks on search results pages, while GEO, or generative engine optimization, helps your brand appear and get cited inside answers written by AI tools. Most of the groundwork is shared, since AI tools rely on crawled, trusted pages. What changes is the goal, the measurement and the amount of off-site work.

What stays the same

Crawling and indexing still come first. If a page is blocked, slow or hidden behind JavaScript, neither Google nor an AI crawler will use it. Quality still matters, because AI tools favour clear, accurate, well-sourced content. And trust still matters, since both search engines and models prefer brands that the wider web describes consistently.

So a strong SEO base is the best GEO head start. Our technical SEO checklist covers that base.

What changes with GEO

First, the unit of success shifts. In SEO, a page ranks. In GEO, a brand gets named, sometimes with no link at all. That means unlinked mentions, reviews and forum threads matter more than before.

Second, the writing style tightens. AI tools lift short passages, so each section should start with a direct answer, as explained in our guide to answer-first content.

Third, measurement needs new tools. Rank trackers do not show AI answers, so you need a fixed prompt list and a monthly check. Our guide to AI search tracking tools compares the options.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. Search engines still send most website traffic, and AI tools often draw on the same pages that rank. In fact, Gemini and AI Mode use Google Search directly, while ChatGPT search and Perplexity run their own retrieval over web results. A brand that drops SEO to chase GEO usually loses both.

How to run GEO and SEO together

  1. Keep one keyword and question plan that serves both.
  2. Write pages answer-first so they rank and get quoted.
  3. Turn link building into a broader mention-building plan.
  4. Report rankings and AI visibility side by side each month.
  5. Review which AI sources cite competitors, then target those sources.

Budget: GEO vs SEO

For most businesses, GEO is an extension of an SEO budget rather than a new line. Expect to move some spend from pure link building to PR, original research and community work. Meanwhile, the content and technical work you already pay for serves both goals.
