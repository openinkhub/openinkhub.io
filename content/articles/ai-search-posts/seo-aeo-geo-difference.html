The SEO, AEO, GEO difference, plus AIO, in one line each: SEO wins rankings on search results pages, AEO wins the direct answer in snippets and voice replies, GEO wins mentions and citations inside AI-generated answers, and AIO makes the whole site easy for AI systems to crawl, read and trust. They stack on top of each other, so most businesses need a mix rather than a choice.

The four layers explained

SEO (search engine optimization) is the base. It covers crawling, indexing, content, links and page experience. Without it, the other three struggle.

AEO (answer engine optimization) sits on top of SEO. It shapes pages so engines can quote a short answer. See what AEO is for detail.

GEO (generative engine optimization) reaches beyond your site. It builds the brand signals AI tools use when they write answers. Read what GEO is for the full picture.

AIO (AI optimization) supports all of them. It covers AI crawler access, clean HTML, structured data and agent readiness. Our glossary on what AIO is explains both meanings of the term.

How the SEO, AEO, GEO difference plays out in practice

Take one page: "How much does a website cost in India?". SEO makes it rank. AEO puts a clear price range in the first lines so Google can show it as a snippet. GEO earns mentions of your brand in articles and forums about website costs. Meanwhile, AIO makes sure AI crawlers can fetch the page and read the price table in plain HTML.

So one topic touches all four layers. That is why splitting them into separate projects wastes effort.

Common mistakes

  • Chasing GEO tactics while key pages are not indexed.
  • Adding FAQ blocks everywhere and calling it AEO.
  • Blocking all AI crawlers by default, then wondering why AI tools ignore you.
  • Reporting each layer separately, so nobody sees the full picture.
