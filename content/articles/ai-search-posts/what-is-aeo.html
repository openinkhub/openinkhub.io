What is AEO? AEO, or answer engine optimization, is the work of shaping your pages so that search engines, voice assistants and AI tools can lift a direct answer from them. Instead of only chasing a blue link, you aim to be the sentence that Google shows in a featured snippet, the reply a voice assistant reads out, or the source an AI summary quotes.

So the short answer to "what is AEO" is this: it is SEO with one extra goal. Your page should not just rank; it should also contain a clear, quotable answer to the exact question a person asked.

Why AEO matters now

Search has changed shape. Many results pages now open with an AI Overview, a snippet or a People Also Ask box, so the first thing a buyer reads is often an answer, not a list of sites. If that answer comes from a competitor, you lose attention before the click even happens.

Meanwhile, tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answer questions in full sentences and cite a handful of sources. Answer engine optimization raises the odds that your page is one of them. AEO examples you see every day

  • A featured snippet that answers "how long does a GST refund take" in two sentences.
  • A voice reply on a smart speaker that reads a shop's opening hours.
  • An AI Overview that lists five steps and links to the page it took them from.
  • A People Also Ask box that expands to show a short paragraph from one site.

In each case, one page wins because it gave the clearest short answer. That is the core of AEO.

How AEO differs from SEO

SEO asks, "Can this page rank for the query?" AEO asks a second question: "Once it ranks, can an engine pull a complete answer from it in one piece?" The two overlap heavily, because an engine rarely quotes a page it does not trust or cannot crawl.

In practice, AEO adds a layer of writing and structure on top of SEO. You lead with the answer, use headings that match real questions, keep key facts in short paragraphs, lists or tables, and add FAQ sections where they help. The building blocks of answer engine optimization

First, research the questions your buyers ask. Sales calls, support tickets and the People Also Ask box are better sources than any keyword list; our guide to question research for SEO shows how to collect them.

Next, write answer-first content. Each section opens with a 40 to 60 word answer, then adds detail. After that, format for extraction: numbered steps for processes, tables for comparisons and short definitions for terms.

Finally, support the page with clean technical basics and structured data where it is valid. The goal is a page that a crawler can read quickly and a person can trust.

How to start with AEO this month

Pick ten pages that already rank on page one for question-style searches. For each one, add a direct answer under the main heading, tighten the headings into questions and add three to five genuine FAQs. Then track which pages win snippets or appear in AI answers. What AEO is not

AEO is not a trick, a plugin or a special tag. No markup forces an engine to quote you. Instead, it is steady editorial work: clear questions, honest answers and pages that deserve to be the source. Teams that treat it that way see the best results.
