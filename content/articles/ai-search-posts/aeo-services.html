AEO services, or answer engine optimization services, help your pages become the direct answer that Google, voice assistants and AI tools show for buyer questions. A good package includes question research, answer-first rewrites of key pages, FAQ sections, structured data, technical fixes and monthly tracking of snippets and AI answers. Most providers price AEO as part of a monthly SEO plan rather than as a separate product.

What good AEO services include

WorkWhat it involves
Question researchCollecting real buyer questions from search data, sales and support
Page mappingAssigning one main question to each page, so pages do not compete
Answer-first rewritesAdding direct 40 to 60 word answers under question headings
FormattingTurning steps into lists and comparisons into tables
FAQ sectionsWriting three to eight genuine follow-up questions per key page
Structured dataAdding valid Article, Organization and other schema
Technical checksIndexing, speed, rendering and AI crawler access
TrackingSnippets held, question query clicks and AI answer mentions

If a proposal skips research or tracking, it is probably a content-writing package with an AEO label. Our guide to how to do AEO shows the full process a provider should follow.

How AEO services pricing works

What results to expect

  • Weeks 2 to 6: pages that already rank on page one start winning featured snippets after rewrites.
  • Months 2 to 4: question query impressions and clicks rise in Search Console.
  • Months 3 to 6: AI Overviews and assistants begin citing improved pages more often.

These timelines vary by competition and by how strong your site already is. No provider can guarantee a snippet or an AI citation, because search engines decide what to show.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. How will you find the questions my buyers ask?
  2. Which pages will you change first, and why?
  3. Will you rewrite pages yourselves, or only send recommendations?
  4. How do you track snippets and AI answer mentions?
  5. What does the monthly report include?
  6. How do you handle technical issues you find?
  7. Can I see an example of an answer-first rewrite?

Red flags

  • Guaranteed snippets, AI citations or rankings.
  • Adding FAQ schema to every page and calling it AEO.
  • No access to Search Console data or clear reports.
  • Long lock-in contracts before any results.
