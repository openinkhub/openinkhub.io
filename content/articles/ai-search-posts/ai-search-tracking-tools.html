AI search tracking tools run a set of prompts in assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot on a schedule, then report how often your brand is mentioned or cited and which competitors appear beside you. They are useful for spotting trends across many prompts and markets. However, AI answers vary between runs, so treat their numbers as averages, not exact counts.

What these tools measure

  • Mention rate: the share of prompt runs where your brand is named.
  • Citation share: how often your site is linked as a source.
  • Share of voice: your mentions compared with competitors'.
  • Sentiment: whether descriptions of your brand are positive, neutral or negative.
  • Source domains: which sites the assistants cite for your topics.
  • Google AI Overviews presence: whether you appear in overviews for tracked keywords.

Types of tools available

The market moves fast, so check current features before you buy. Broadly, options fall into three groups:

  1. Established SEO suites with AI modules. Tools such as Semrush, Ahrefs, SE Ranking and Moz have added AI visibility or AI Overview tracking to their platforms. They suit teams that already use the suite.
  2. Dedicated AI visibility trackers. Newer tools focus only on prompt tracking across assistants, often with deeper source analysis.
  3. Do-it-yourself tracking. A spreadsheet, a fixed prompt list and a monthly manual check. Free, slower, but fully under your control.

Pick based on budget, number of markets and how often you need data. For most small businesses, a monthly manual check or an add-on to an existing suite is enough.

How to choose AI search tracking tools

Start with your prompt set. If you have 30 prompts and four engines, a spreadsheet works. Once you reach hundreds of prompts or several countries, a tool saves time. Then ask each vendor how many times it runs each prompt, whether it uses logged-out sessions and how it handles location. Those answers tell you how reliable its numbers are.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Answers change between runs, accounts and days.
  • Logged-in, personalised results may differ from what tools see.
  • Some assistants restrict automated access, which affects coverage.
  • Mention counts do not show revenue on their own.

A DIY tracking method

If you prefer to start free, run your prompts by hand each month in a clean browser session. Log mentions, citations, competitors and sources in a sheet. Then chart your mention rate over time. This method also teaches you how each assistant behaves, which helps when you later choose a paid tool.

Report the results
