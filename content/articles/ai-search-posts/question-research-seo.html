Question research SEO is the process of finding the exact questions your buyers ask, in their own words, so you can answer them on your site. The best sources are People Also Ask boxes, Google Search Console, sales calls, support tickets and community threads on Reddit or Quora. Together they show what people really want to know before they buy.

Good question research SEO beats guesswork. It also gives you the raw material for answer engine optimization, FAQs and AI search visibility.

Why questions matter more than keywords

Keyword tools show short phrases and volumes. Buyers, however, ask full questions, especially in voice search and AI chats. A question such as "can I switch SEO agencies without losing rankings" reveals intent, worry and stage of the journey in one line. That detail helps you write a page that truly fits.

Also, AI tools answer questions, not keywords. If your page answers the question clearly, it has a better chance of being quoted. See what AEO is for the wider picture.

Source 1: People Also Ask

Search your main topic and open the People Also Ask box. Each click reveals more questions. Note the wording and the type of answer Google shows, whether a paragraph, a list or a table. Repeat for five to ten core topics.

Source 2: Google Search Console

In the Performance report, filter queries with a regular expression such as ^(what|how|why|which|can|does|is)\b. This shows question searches that already bring you impressions. Pages with high impressions but low clicks are quick wins. Our guide to Search Console for AEO explains the filters.

Source 3: Sales calls and support tickets

Ask your sales and support teams for the ten questions they hear most. These often never appear in tools, because they are specific to your market. For example, a web agency may hear "will I own the website code?" again and again. That is a perfect page or FAQ topic.

Source 4: Reddit, Quora and reviews

Community threads show the questions people ask peers, plus the doubts they have about providers. Search Reddit for your service and city, and read the replies too. Reviews on Google and marketplaces also reveal what buyers care about. Our article on why Reddit wins AI citations explains why these threads matter for AI answers.

How to organize the questions

Put every question in a sheet with these columns: question, source, stage (learn, compare, buy), intent, matching page and status. Then group close variants together. Next, pick one main question per page and three to eight follow-ups for its FAQ section.
