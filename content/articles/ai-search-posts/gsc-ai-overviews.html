GSC AI Overviews data is not reported on its own: Google Search Console includes clicks and impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode in the Web search type of the Performance report, without a separate filter. Still, Search Console is the best free tool for AEO and GEO reporting. With question filters, page groups and a fixed query list, you can track how your answers perform across Google's AI and classic results.

What Search Console shows about AI features

Google's guide to AI features and your website confirms that AI Overviews and AI Mode traffic counts toward the Web search type. So when a page is cited in an overview and someone clicks, that click appears in your normal Performance report. However, you cannot split it out from regular clicks there.

As a result, GSC AI Overviews reporting works best when you combine Search Console data with your own list of queries that show overviews.

Useful filters for AEO reporting

  • Question queries: use a custom regex filter such as ^(what|how|why|which|when|who|can|does|is|are)\b.
  • Long queries: use a regex like ^(\S+\s){6,}\S+$ to find queries of seven or more words, which are often conversational.
  • Page groups: filter by URL path, such as /blog/ or a service folder.
  • Device: compare mobile and desktop, since voice and AI use differ by device.

Build a fixed AI Overview query list

  1. Search your top 50 target queries and note which show an AI Overview.
  2. Record whether your page is cited in each overview.
  3. Save those queries in a sheet with their Search Console impressions, clicks and position.
  4. Repeat the check every month on the same queries.

This list gives you a GSC AI Overviews view that Search Console alone cannot. In addition, it shows whether citations rise after you rewrite pages answer-first; see Google AI Overviews optimization for the method.

Reading the trends carefully

When an AI Overview appears for a query, clicks may fall even if your position holds, because some people get their answer on the page. So look at impressions and position alongside clicks. Rising impressions with steady clicks can still mean more visibility. Our guide to zero-click search covers how to explain this to stakeholders.

Limits of Search Console

  • No separate AI Overview or AI Mode filter.
  • Data is sampled and anonymised for rare queries.
  • Only 16 months of history is kept.
