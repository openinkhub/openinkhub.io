A zero-click search is a search that ends without the person clicking any result, because the answer appears right on the results page in a snippet, a map pack, a knowledge panel or an AI Overview. Zero-click search is growing as engines answer more questions directly. So success can no longer be measured by clicks alone; you also need to track visibility, brand demand and actions taken from the results page.

Why zero-click searches are rising

Search engines want to answer quickly. For simple questions, such as weather, conversions or opening hours, the answer fits on the page. In addition, AI Overviews and AI Mode now summarise many complex topics, and assistants like ChatGPT answer without a results page at all. As a result, more journeys start and end without a website visit.

Is zero-click search bad for business?

Not always. When your brand is the answer, people still see your name, your rating or your phone number. Many then search for you later, call you or visit your shop. That value is real, even though it does not show as an organic click.

However, if competitors own those answer spots, you lose attention before the click. So the aim is to be the visible answer, not to fight the format. Our guide to featured snippet optimization explains how.

How to report zero-click success

First, group your target queries by intent: quick answers, research and purchase. Then report impressions and answer visibility for the quick-answer group, and clicks and leads for the purchase group. Next, show branded search trends next to those numbers. Leadership can then see that visibility today drives demand tomorrow.

Content that still earns clicks

Some questions cannot be answered in two lines. Detailed guides, calculators, comparisons, case studies and local service pages still draw clicks, because people need more than a summary. So shift effort toward content with depth, while keeping a short answer at the top for the zero-click audience.

Local zero-click search

For local businesses, the map pack is often the whole journey. People read reviews, check hours and tap "call" without visiting your site. Therefore, a complete, accurate Google Business Profile matters as much as your website. Our Google Business Profile service keeps it in shape.

What to change in your strategy

  • Track impressions and brand demand, not only clicks.
  • Write short answers that carry your brand name where it fits naturally.
  • Invest in pages with depth that still need a visit.
  • Measure AI mentions alongside search rankings.
