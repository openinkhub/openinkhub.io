What is GEO? GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the work that helps AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot find, trust and mention your brand when they write an answer. Classic SEO aims for a ranking on a results page. GEO aims for a place inside the answer itself, as a named brand, a cited source or both.

The term came from a 2023 research paper by Princeton and partners, which tested how content changes affect visibility in AI-generated answers. Since then, it has become shorthand for AI search visibility work.

How generative engines choose sources

Most AI assistants now search the web for current questions. They fetch a set of pages, read them, and write an answer that blends what they found. Some show citations; others only name brands. In addition, the model's training data shapes which brands it already "knows" for a topic.

So there are two routes into an answer. One is live retrieval, where your page is fetched and cited. The other is model memory, where your brand appears because the web talks about it often and consistently. GEO works on both.

The main levers of generative engine optimization

  • Crawlable, clear pages: AI crawlers must reach your content, and its key facts must be easy to extract.
  • Entity consistency: your name, services, location and founders should match everywhere, as covered in our guide to entity SEO.
  • Brand mentions: reviews, articles, lists and forum threads that name you with your topic; see brand mentions in LLM answers.
  • Original data: facts that only you publish, which others then cite.
  • Community presence: genuine activity on platforms AI tools often cite, such as Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn.

What is GEO not?

GEO is not a replacement for SEO. AI tools lean heavily on search indexes and trusted pages, so weak SEO caps your GEO results. It is also not about tricking a model with hidden text or repeated phrases. Those tactics break search spam rules and rarely work for long.

Who needs GEO?

Any business whose buyers ask AI tools for advice before they buy. That now includes B2B services, software, education, healthcare, travel and many local services. If a buyer types "best accounting software for a small firm in India" into ChatGPT, the brands in that answer win attention before any website visit.

How to measure GEO

First, build a fixed list of buyer prompts, as described in our guide to the AI prompt set. Then run those prompts in each AI tool every month. Record whether your brand is mentioned, whether you are cited, how you are described and which competitors appear. Over time, that gives you a share of voice figure you can report.

Also, track AI referral visits in analytics. Our guide to tracking AI traffic in GA4 shows the setup.

A simple GEO starting plan

  1. Check that GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot can reach your key pages.
  2. Fix any mismatched business details across your site and listings.
  3. Rewrite your top pages with clear, answer-first sections.
  4. Run your prompt set and note the sources AI tools cite.
  5. Earn a place in those sources through PR, reviews and expert content.
