An AI visibility dashboard brings your SEO, AEO and GEO numbers onto one page, so you can see each month whether search engines and AI assistants are showing, quoting and recommending your brand. The core metrics are prompt mention rate, citation share, AI referral visits and leads, question query performance in Search Console and branded search trends.

Why one dashboard beats separate reports

SEO, AEO and GEO share most of the same work. If each has its own report, however, nobody sees the full effect. Instead, a single dashboard shows how rankings, answers and AI mentions move together, which makes decisions easier. It also keeps leadership focused on a few clear numbers.

Keep the first page to these eight numbers. After that, detail can go on later pages.

Where each number comes from

  • Prompt metrics: from a frozen list of prompts, described in our guide to the AI prompt set.
  • AI visits and leads: from a custom channel group, set up as in AI traffic in GA4.
  • Question queries: from Search Console regex filters, explained in Search Console for AEO reporting.
  • Snippets: also from monthly manual checks on target queries.

Dashboard layout

  1. Summary (page 1): the eight metrics as scorecards, each with the change from last month.
  2. AI visibility (page 2): mention rate by tool and against top competitors.
  3. Search (page 3): question queries, snippets and branded search trends.
  4. Traffic and leads (page 4): AI and organic sessions, conversions and top landing pages.
  5. Actions (page 5): what changed, why, and next month's plan.

Which tool to build it in

Looker Studio is free and connects directly to GA4 and Search Console. For prompt data, use a Google Sheet as a data source too and update it monthly. If you use a paid AI tracking tool, check whether it exports to Sheets or has a Looker Studio connector. Our comparison of AI search tracking tools covers export options. In addition, Google's Looker Studio help explains how to connect sources.

Tips for a dashboard people use

  • Show trends over at least six months, not single values.
  • Also add a one-line note under each chart explaining the change.
  • Use the same colours for the same metric everywhere.
  • Avoid vanity numbers that do not lead to a decision.
  • Then review the dashboard in a short monthly meeting.

Linking the dashboard to ROI

Once the dashboard runs for a few months, add a simple return estimate: leads from AI and organic search, multiplied by close rate and deal value, compared with the cost of the work. Our guide to AI search ROI explains the method, so you can start simply. Over time, the AI visibility dashboard becomes the place where leadership sees search value at a glance.
