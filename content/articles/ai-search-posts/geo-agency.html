A GEO agency helps your brand appear, and appear accurately, in answers from AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot. To choose one, look for a clear process that covers prompt tracking, content changes, entity consistency and mention building, plus honest reporting. Avoid any agency that guarantees AI citations, because no one controls how AI systems pick sources.

What a GEO agency should do

  • Baseline: run a fixed set of buyer prompts across AI tools and record where you appear.
  • Technical access: check that AI crawlers can reach your pages and read them without JavaScript.
  • Content: rewrite key pages answer-first, with clear facts, tables and sources.
  • Entities: align your brand details across your site, profiles and directories.
  • Mentions: earn fair places in the lists, reviews and articles AI tools cite.
  • Reporting: show mention rate, citations and AI referral traffic each month.

12 questions to ask a GEO agency

  1. How do you build and freeze the prompt set you track?
  2. Which AI tools do you monitor, and how often?
  3. How many times do you run each prompt, given that answers vary?
  4. What did you change for a past client, and what moved?
  5. How do you decide which pages to rewrite first?
  6. How do you handle robots.txt rules for AI crawlers?
  7. What is your approach to brand mentions and digital PR?
  8. Do you work on Reddit, YouTube or LinkedIn, and how?
  9. How do you track AI referral traffic in analytics?
  10. Who on your team does the work, and what is their background?
  11. What does the monthly report look like?
  12. Do you also handle SEO, or will we need another agency?

Red flags to avoid

  • Guaranteed placement in ChatGPT or AI Overviews.
  • Hidden text, prompt injection or other tricks aimed at AI crawlers.
  • Mass-produced pages or fake reviews to "train" AI models.
  • Reports full of screenshots but no consistent metrics.
  • No mention of SEO basics such as indexing and speed.

Many of these tactics also break search spam policies. Our article on AI search myths lists more claims to question.

Should your GEO agency also do SEO?

Ideally, yes. AI tools rely heavily on search indexes and trusted pages, so GEO and SEO share most of their groundwork. One team can plan content, technical fixes and mentions together, which avoids gaps and duplicate effort. Our comparison of GEO vs SEO explains the overlap.
