Does FAQ schema still work? FAQ schema, the FAQPage structured data type, still works as valid markup, but since August 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results only for a small group of well-known government and health websites. For most businesses, it no longer changes how a listing looks. Even so, the questions and answers behind the markup keep their value for readers, long-tail searches and AI answers.

What changed for FAQ schema

For years, adding FAQ schema could expand a search listing with drop-down questions. That made results bigger and more eye-catching, so many sites added it everywhere. Then, in August 2023, Google announced it would limit FAQ rich results to authoritative government and health sites, and it stopped showing HowTo rich results altogether. Google explained the change in its post on simplifying search results.

So if your listing lost its FAQ drop-downs, that is why. It does not mean your markup broke.

What the evidence says now

  • Rich results: rare for normal business sites, as described above.
  • Rankings: structured data is not a direct ranking factor, so removing or adding FAQ schema does not move rankings on its own.
  • Understanding: markup still helps machines read a page's question and answer structure.
  • Other engines: Bing and other tools may still read FAQPage data in their own ways.

In short, FAQ schema has moved from a visual trick to a minor helper. The visible content is what counts.

Should you keep or remove FAQ schema?

Keep it where it is valid and matches visible content. It does no harm, and it may help some systems. However, do not spend hours adding it to every page, and never mark up questions that are not shown on the page. Google's structured data policies require markup to reflect what users see.

Also, remove FAQ markup from pages where the FAQ block is the same across the site. Repeated boilerplate adds no value.

Why FAQ content still matters

The real asset was always the content, not the code. A clear FAQ section answers doubts just before a buyer decides. It also helps a page match long question searches, and it gives AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity short, quotable answers. How to add FAQ schema correctly

  1. Write three to eight real questions and answers on the page.
  2. Add FAQPage JSON-LD with the same wording as the visible text.
  3. Test it with Google's Rich Results Test or the Schema Markup Validator.
  4. Avoid marking up questions written by users; that is QAPage territory.
  5. Update the markup whenever you edit the answers.

What to focus on instead

Rather than chasing rich results, put effort into answer-first writing, strong headings and up-to-date facts. In addition, make sure your FAQ answers agree with your pricing and service pages, because AI tools notice contradictions. These steps help every search surface, not just one rich result type.
