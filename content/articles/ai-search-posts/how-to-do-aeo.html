How to do AEO: list the questions your buyers ask, match each one to a page, open that page with a short direct answer, structure the rest for easy extraction, add genuine FAQs and valid schema, fix crawl and speed basics, then track where your answers appear. Those seven steps cover most of answer engine optimization for a small or mid-sized site.

Below, each step comes with the practical detail you need to act on it this week.

Step 1: Collect real questions

Start with what people actually ask, not with a keyword tool. Look at sales call notes, support tickets, WhatsApp chats, People Also Ask boxes and the question queries in Google Search Console. Aim for 30 to 50 questions across the buying journey. Our guide to question research for SEO lists the best sources.

Step 2: Map one main question to one page

Next, decide which page should answer each question. One page can handle a main question plus a few close follow-ups. However, two pages chasing the same question will compete, so merge or refocus them; our article on keyword cannibalization shows how.

Step 3: Write the answer first

Under the main heading, give a complete answer in 40 to 60 words. Use the words of the question, state the fact plainly and avoid a slow wind-up. Then add context, examples and limits below it. This pattern, explained in our guide to answer-first content, is the heart of how to do AEO well.

Step 4: Structure for extraction

Engines quote content that is easy to lift. So match the format to the question:

  • Processes become numbered steps.
  • Comparisons become tables with clear column names.
  • Definitions become one or two plain sentences.
  • Options become short bulleted lists.

Also, use headings phrased like the questions people type. Our guide to featured snippet optimization goes deeper on formats.

Step 5: Add FAQs and schema

Add three to eight real follow-up questions near the end of the page. Keep each answer short and specific. Then add valid structured data, such as Article, Organization and BreadcrumbList. FAQ rich results now show for very few sites, yet clear question and answer blocks still help readers and AI tools; see does FAQ schema still work for the detail.

Step 6: Fix the technical basics

An answer engine cannot quote a page it cannot fetch. Check that key pages are indexable, load fast on mobile and show their main text in the raw HTML. In addition, review which AI crawlers your robots.txt allows. Our technical SEO checklist covers each item.

Step 7: Track and repeat

Finally, measure results every month. In Search Console, filter for question queries and watch impressions and clicks. Note which pages win featured snippets. Then run your main questions in AI tools and record whether they cite you. Our guide to Search Console reporting for AEO gives a simple template.

After that, repeat the cycle with the next batch of questions. AEO is not a one-off project; buyer questions change, and so do the pages that answer them best.

How long does it take?

Once you know how to do AEO, the timing is fairly predictable. Pages that already rank can win snippets within a few weeks of an answer-first rewrite. New topics take longer, often three to six months, because the page must first earn rankings and trust. Google's guide to creating helpful content is worth reading before you begin.
