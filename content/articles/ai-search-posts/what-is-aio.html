What is AIO? In search marketing, AIO most often means AI optimization: the work that makes a website easy for AI systems to crawl, read, trust and recommend. However, people also use "AIO" as shorthand for Google AI Overviews, the AI summaries at the top of some results pages. The two meanings are linked, but they are not the same thing.

This short glossary explains both meanings, the types of AIO work, and how to tell which one someone means.

Meaning 1: AIO as AI optimization

AI optimization, sometimes written as artificial intelligence optimization, is a set of technical and content practices. Its aim is simple. When an AI crawler, assistant or agent reaches your site, it should find clean content, clear facts about your business and nothing that blocks it.

So AIO sits close to technical SEO, but it looks at a wider group of visitors. Besides Googlebot, you now have GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and browser agents that act for users. Our AI optimization service covers this work end to end.

Meaning 2: AIO as AI Overviews

Google AI Overviews are AI-written summaries that appear above or among normal results for some searches. They include links to the pages they draw on. When someone says "we lost traffic to AIO", they usually mean this feature.

Optimizing for AI Overviews is really a mix of SEO and AEO. Google says there is no special markup for it. For the practical steps, read our guide to Google AI Overviews optimization.

Types of AIO work

  • Crawler access: deciding which AI bots may fetch your pages, set out in our guide to AI crawlers and robots.txt.
  • Readable HTML: making sure key text is present without JavaScript, as explained in JavaScript SEO for AI.
  • Structured data: describing your organization, authors and articles with valid schema.
  • Entity clarity: keeping your brand facts identical across your site and the wider web.
  • Content quality: reviewing AI-assisted content so it stays accurate and useful.
  • Agent readiness: giving forms and buttons clear labels so AI agents can use them.

AIO vs AEO vs GEO

AEO focuses on answers inside pages. GEO focuses on how AI tools mention your brand across the web. AIO, meanwhile, focuses on the site itself: access, structure and trust signals that every AI system relies on. In other words, AIO is the foundation the other two stand on.

How to tell which AIO someone means

When a client asks "what is AIO in this report?", context usually settles it. If the talk is about rankings, clicks or the top of a results page, AIO probably means AI Overviews. If it is about bots, schema, site structure or agents, AIO means AI optimization. When in doubt, ask, because the fixes differ.

Where to start with AI optimization

First, check your robots.txt and firewall to see which AI crawlers you allow. Next, view the raw HTML of your top pages to confirm the main text is there. Then add Organization and Article schema, and fix any mismatched business details. Google's guide to AI features and your website is a useful reference while you work.
