Entity SEO is the work of helping search engines and AI tools understand your brand as a distinct "thing", with clear facts and links to related people, places and topics. When the same details about your business appear on your site, your profiles and trusted third-party sources, engines connect them into a single entity. That makes it easier for them to show your brand in knowledge panels, local results and AI answers.

What is an entity?

An entity is anything a search engine can identify on its own: a company, a person, a product, a city or an idea. Google stores facts about entities in its Knowledge Graph, along with the links between them. For example, "Openink Hub" might link to "Kolkata", "SEO services" and its founder. Google's own post on things, not strings explains the idea.

AI assistants use a similar view. When they describe a brand, they rely on facts that many sources agree on.

Why entity SEO matters for AI search

If your website says you serve five cities, your Google Business Profile lists two and a directory still shows an old address, engines get mixed signals. As a result, an AI tool may describe you wrongly or skip you. Consistent facts, by contrast, give engines confidence. That is why entity work sits at the core of generative engine optimization.

Steps to build your knowledge graph footprint

  1. Write the core facts. Legal name, brand name, founding year, founders, address, phone, services and areas served.
  2. Publish a strong About page. State those facts in plain text, with team bios and contact details.
  3. Add Organization schema. Include name, logo, URL, contact point and sameAs links to your official profiles. Our schema markup guide shows the code.
  4. Align every profile. Update Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Facebook, Crunchbase and key directories so they match.
  5. Mark up people. Use Person schema on author and founder pages, with links to their profiles.
  6. Earn third-party mentions. News articles, interviews and industry lists confirm the facts from outside.
  7. Check results. Search your brand name and see what the panel, the map and AI tools say.

The role of sameAs links

The sameAs property in schema tells engines, "this profile is also us". It ties your website to your LinkedIn page, YouTube channel and other official accounts. Only list profiles you control, and keep them active. Otherwise, a dead or wrong profile adds noise rather than clarity.

Can you request a Knowledge Panel?

There is no form to create one. Google shows a panel when it is confident about an entity. However, once a panel exists, you can claim it and suggest edits. Meanwhile, the best way to earn one is steady, consistent facts across trusted sources, plus genuine coverage.

Entity SEO for local businesses

For local firms, the Google Business Profile is the most important entity source. Keep the name, category, address and hours exact. Then make your location pages match those details word for word. Our Google Business Profile service handles this alignment.

Common entity mistakes

  • Using slightly different brand names across platforms.
  • Listing old phone numbers or addresses in directories.
  • Adding sameAs links to profiles you do not own.
  • Forgetting author pages, so content has no clear expert behind it.
